Meet the Agents

Each agent is a complete, hosted AI system — configured for your business and managed by us.

Inbox Agent

Email · CRM · n8n

Your inbox, handled intelligently — 24/7.

The Inbox Agent reads every incoming email, classifies it by intent (sales inquiry, support request, invoice, spam) and routes it to the right person or system. For common queries it drafts a reply using your tone and templates, ready for one-click send. Everything is logged to your CRM automatically.

  • Intelligent classification: Sorts emails by intent, priority and sender type.
  • Draft generation: AI-written replies matching your voice and templates.
  • CRM sync: Auto-logs interactions to Salesforce, HubSpot or any CRM.
  • Human escalation: Routes complex or sensitive threads to the right team member.
Saves ~8h/week for most teams Get This Agent

Sales Assistant Agent

Salesforce · n8n · LLM

Your pipeline — always warm. Always moving.

The Sales Assistant keeps your deals from going cold. It monitors your CRM for stale opportunities, writes personalised follow-up emails, logs call notes automatically and sets reminders for the next step. You focus on closing; the agent handles the admin.

  • Stale deal alerts: Flags opportunities that haven't moved in X days.
  • Auto follow-ups: Writes and schedules personalised outreach emails.
  • Call note summarisation: Transcribes and summarises calls, pushes notes to CRM.
  • Next-step reminders: Automated task creation so nothing slips through.
Saves ~6h/week per sales rep Get This Agent

XML/EDI Monitor Agent

FTP · SFTP · XML · EDI

Silent watchdog for your file exchange — instant alert on every error.

The XML/EDI Monitor Agent watches your FTP/SFTP server around the clock. When a new file arrives it validates the schema, parses the content, flags anomalies (duplicate orders, unexpected values, missing fields) and pushes clean data to your ERP or CRM. If anything fails, you get an instant Slack or email alert with the exact error and file location.

  • FTP/SFTP watcher: Polls or event-triggers on new file arrival.
  • Schema validation: Checks against EDIFACT, X12, UBL or your custom XML schema.
  • Anomaly detection: AI flags statistical outliers and data quality issues.
  • ERP/CRM push: Delivers parsed data to SAP, Salesforce, Odoo or any REST API.
  • Instant alerting: Slack, email or webhook notification on any parse error.
Eliminates hours of manual checking/week Get This Agent

AI Readiness Agent

Strategy · Audit · Report

Know exactly where AI fits in your business — scored, ranked, actionable.

The AI Readiness Agent guides your team through a structured questionnaire covering your workflows, data maturity, team skills and strategic goals. It analyses the responses, scores your AI readiness across five dimensions, ranks your highest-ROI opportunities and delivers a written action plan with tool recommendations and budget estimates.

  • Guided audit questionnaire: 20–30 targeted questions, 15 minutes to complete.
  • 5-dimension scoring: Data, Process, People, Technology and Strategy.
  • Opportunity ranking: Prioritised list of automation/AI use cases by ROI and effort.
  • Written action plan: PDF report with tool recommendations and budget ranges.
Replaces a €5,000 consulting engagement Get This Agent

How do the agents work technically?

Each agent is a pre-configured n8n workflow + AI model stack, hosted and managed by us. You connect your accounts (email, CRM, FTP, etc.) via guided setup, and the agent starts working immediately. No coding required.

Can I customise the agents for my specific workflow?

Yes. All plans include configuration options (tone, templates, routing rules). On Growth and Scale plans you can request custom logic changes. We manage updates and improvements as part of the subscription.

What does the subscription include?

Hosting, monitoring, updates, bug fixes and support are all included in every plan. There are no hidden infrastructure costs. On Scale plan, you also get a monthly optimisation call.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We process data in EU-based infrastructure and follow GDPR best practices. Credentials are stored encrypted and never shared. We can provide a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) on request.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime with 30 days notice. We'll export your configuration so you or another provider can continue running the agent if needed. No lock-in.
