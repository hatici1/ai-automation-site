About Us

Learn about our mission and the people behind AI Automation & Engineering Solutions.

Our Mission

We believe that small and medium‑sized businesses should have access to the same sophisticated automation and AI tools as large enterprises. Our mission is to democratise technology by building workflows, intelligent systems and AI strategies that are affordable, transparent and easy to use. From visual automation with n8n and modern machine learning to XML/EDI pipelines and AI counseling, we help organisations streamline operations, gain insights and create new value.

Who We Are

Founded by Hasan Atici, an AI counselor and automation engineer, AI Solutions grew out of a passion for connecting systems and making technology work for people. With a background in software development, data science, business process optimisation and legacy system integration, Hasan has spent years helping clients across industries automate repetitive tasks, integrate FTP/XML/EDI pipelines, deploy RAG chatbots and build intelligent products. We take pride in being transparent about our methods and delivering solutions that fit your needs — whether that's a €500 AI audit or a full custom ML deployment.

Our Approach

We follow best practices in responsive design, accessibility and user experience. By taking a mobile‑first approach, using fluid grids and flexible images, and ensuring adequate contrast and keyboard navigation, we build web interfaces that are friendly on any device. In our AI projects we adhere to a structured machine learning process involving problem exploration, data engineering, model engineering and ML Ops. This helps us deliver reliable, maintainable solutions.
