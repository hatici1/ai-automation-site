n8n Workflow Automation

400+ integrations. Infinite possibilities.

n8n is a flexible automation platform that lets you build sophisticated workflows without limits on logic. It provides a visual editor for fast iteration where you see results instantly. The platform comes with over 400 pre‑configured integrations, so you can automate tasks across your favourite apps without writing boilerplate code. You can also fall back to custom code when you need full control and easily merge, loop or filter your data.

We design and build n8n workflows tailored to your business. Whether you're capturing leads, synchronising data between systems, processing XML files or building complex AI agent pipelines, we'll create reliable automation that scales as your needs grow.

  • Visual workflow design: Build and iterate quickly using n8n's visual editor.

XML / EDI & FTP Integration

Bridge your legacy systems to the modern world.

Many businesses still run critical operations on FTP/SFTP servers exchanging XML or EDI files — order confirmations, invoices, inventory feeds, shipping notices. These pipelines are fragile, opaque and invisible until something breaks. We bring visibility, reliability and intelligence to your file-based integrations without forcing you to rip and replace your existing infrastructure.

We build automated pipelines that watch your FTP/SFTP servers for new files, parse and validate the content (XML, CSV, EDIFACT, X12, JSON), transform data into the target format, push it to your ERP, CRM or database, and alert you instantly if anything fails. Where appropriate we layer in AI to detect anomalies, classify documents and generate summaries.

  • FTP/SFTP watchers: Scheduled or event-driven pickup of incoming files.
  • XML & EDI parsing: EDIFACT, X12, UBL, custom XML schemas — we handle them all.
  • Data transformation: Map and convert between formats using configurable rules or code.
  • ERP / CRM push: Deliver clean data to SAP, Salesforce, Odoo, or any REST/SOAP API.
  • Error alerting: Real-time Slack, email or webhook notifications on parse failures or schema mismatches.
  • AI anomaly detection: Flag unusual values, duplicate orders or outlier quantities automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an AI counseling session include?

An AI counseling session covers a structured audit of your current workflows, identification of automation and AI opportunities, tool recommendations, a realistic ROI estimate and a prioritised adoption roadmap. Most clients leave with a 3–6 month action plan.

Can you integrate our legacy FTP-based XML/EDI system?

Yes. We specialise in bridging legacy FTP/SFTP servers and XML/EDI file exchange with modern APIs, databases and automation platforms like n8n. We handle parsing, transformation, validation and error alerting.

How long does it take to build an n8n automation?

Most standard automations are designed, built and tested within 1–2 weeks. Complex multi-system integrations may take 3–4 weeks. We provide a clear timeline during the free discovery call.

Do I need technical knowledge to use the automations you build?

No. We document every workflow and provide hands-on training so your team can monitor, manage and extend the automations without any coding knowledge.

What is the minimum budget to get started?

An AI Readiness Audit starts from €500. Workflow automation and integration packages start from €1,200. The free 30-minute consultation helps us scope exactly what you need.

Do you work with businesses outside Germany?

Yes. We work remotely with clients across the DACH region and broader Europe. All engagements are conducted online, so location is no barrier.
